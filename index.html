JobFill – Resume Autofill: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

What the extension does

JobFill fills in online job application forms using details you save in the extension, such as your name, email, phone number, education, skills and work experience, and an optional resume file.

Data we store

The details you enter and the optional resume file are stored only in your browser's local extension storage on your own device. We do not have access to this data.

Data we do not collect or share

  • The extension does not send any data to our servers or to any third party.
  • We do not sell or transfer user data.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, advertising or remote code.
  • The extension reads form field labels on a page only to decide where to place your saved details. Page content is never stored or transmitted.
  • We do not use your data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

Your control

You can edit or clear your saved details at any time in the extension popup, or remove all data by uninstalling the extension.
